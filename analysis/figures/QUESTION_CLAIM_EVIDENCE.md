# Question–claim–evidence audit

Statuses describe the current preliminary dataset, not the ultimate project.

## 1. TH versus DAT anatomical sampling

**Question:** Do the lines sample morphologically and molecularly different DA
populations?

**Current status: strong framework; quantitative support developing.** The 20x
mask-area panel shows a large superficial/deep TH size difference and broadly
overlapping DAT/TH superficial distributions. Immunostaining for TH and CR is
the direct line-identity evidence and should carry the stronger claim. Mask area
alone does not establish subtype identity.

## 2. TH versus DAT 10x odor coding

**Question:** Do dorsal glomerular DA responses differ in specificity,
suppression, and state dependence?

**Current status: answerable, not yet adjudicated.** Coverage and session count
are adequate for session-within-mouse estimation. Primary evidence should be
signed breadth, separate lifetime sparseness, raw response distributions, and
tonic F0. Pairwise geometry is secondary because its estimates are unstable at
the available repeat counts.

## 3. TH subtype-and-depth and compartment at 20x

**Question:** Do superficial small and deep large TH cells—and their somas and
grouped processes—differ in odor and state modulation?

**Current status: answerable but mouse-limited.** Soma size is strong. The
grouped products support separate soma/process analyses. Specificity, signed
temporal AUC, state effects, and pupil/running association remain to be computed
hierarchically. Claims must remain subtype-and-depth, not subtype alone.

## 4. State reorganization of DA activity

**Question:** Does ket/xyl reduce tonic and evoked DA activity and smooth
temporal dynamics?

**Current status: tonic F0 appears strongest; evoked/temporal claims are
suggestive.** QC shows compression and smoothing, but the presentation needs
session/mouse summaries of F0, raw positive/negative AUC, roughness or temporal
variation, and response breadth. Geometry is not required to establish this
claim.

## 5. Thy1 principal-neuron correspondence

**Question:** Do principal-neuron dendrites broaden excitation, lose
suppression, and lose similar-odor separation under anesthesia?

**Current status: signed-response claim is promising but not yet quantified;
geometry is pair-specific and weak overall.** Mixture 39/40 shows the clearest
awake separation and cosine collapse. Evidence for 17/18 is weak and 31/32 is
mixed. The defensible claim is therefore not yet “similar mixtures generally
collapse.” Separate animals permit correspondence, not causality.

## 6. Mixture/lateral-inhibitory computation

**Question:** Are reciprocal mixtures distinguished through spatial or temporal
structure associated with DA recruitment/suppression?

**Current status: hypothesis-generating.** Crossnobis has low precision with
2–5 repeats and is sensitive to low-repeat comparisons. Use >=3 trials per odor
as the exploratory primary floor, >=4 as sensitivity, and show uncertainty.
Confusion matrices, component-versus-mixture structure, signed difference maps,
and time of emergence may be more interpretable. Current data do not demonstrate
a lateral-inhibitory mechanism.

## 7. Awake arousal modulation

**Question:** Does DA suppression covary with pupil/running within odor after
removing the mean odor response?

**Current status: data available, analysis pending.** This can be tested using
within-session, within-odor residual variation and temporal lag. Motion metrics
and pupil validity are required sensitivities. Frame it as arousal association,
not NE mechanism.
